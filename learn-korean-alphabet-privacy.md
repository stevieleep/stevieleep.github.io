# Learn Korean Alphabet Privacy Policy
 
**Last updated: August 22, 2026**
 
Learn Korean Alphabet respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Learn Korean Alphabet application.
 
## Information We Collect
 
Learn Korean Alphabet does not require users to create an account.
 
The app does not request or collect personal information such as:
 
- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Physical addresses
- Precise location
- Contacts
- Photos
- Health information
- Financial information
 
## Learning Progress
 
Learn Korean Alphabet may save learning progress and app preferences locally on the user's device.
 
Locally stored information may include:
 
- Letters marked as learned
- Lesson completion status
- App settings
- Purchase-unlock status used to display the correct content
 
This information is used only to provide app functionality. It is not transmitted to the developer or sold to third parties.
 
Removing the app may remove locally stored learning progress.
 
## In-App Purchases
 
Learn Korean Alphabet offers an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks additional educational content.
 
Purchases are processed through Apple and StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store the user's full payment-card information.
 
Apple may process transaction and account information according to Apple's own privacy policies.
 
## Analytics and Advertising
 
Learn Korean Alphabet does not currently:
 
- Display third-party advertising
- Use advertising identifiers
- Track users across apps or websites
- Sell personal information
- Use third-party analytics services
- Create behavioral advertising profiles
 
## Audio, Images, and Keyboard Input
 
The app contains educational audio, images, videos, handwriting-practice areas, and Korean keyboard exercises.
 
Handwriting and keyboard entries used within the learning exercises are processed locally for app functionality. Learn Korean Alphabet does not transmit this practice content to the developer.
 
The iPhone keyboard is provided and managed by iOS. Users should review the privacy practices of any third-party keyboards installed on their devices.
 
## Children's Privacy
 
Learn Korean Alphabet is an educational app that may be used by learners of different ages.
 
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not include third-party advertising, social networking features, or account creation.
 
A parent or guardian with questions may contact the developer using the information below.
 
## Data Sharing
 
Learn Korean Alphabet does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.
 
Apple may process information necessary to distribute the app, operate the App Store, process purchases, and restore prior purchases.
 
## Data Security
 
Because learning progress is stored locally, users should protect their devices with the security features provided by Apple, such as a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.
 
## Changes to This Privacy Policy
 
This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.
 
Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
