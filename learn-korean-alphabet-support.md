## Frequently Asked Questions
 
### How do I hear a letter, word, or phrase?
 
Tap the blue speaker icon on the applicable learning card.
 
Make sure your device volume is turned up if no sound plays.
 
### How do I clear my handwriting practice?
 
Tap the pink eraser button in the drawing area. This removes your practice strokes without removing the underlying stroke-order guide.
 
### How do I add the Korean keyboard to my iPhone?
 
Open **Settings**, tap **General**, select **Keyboard**, tap **Keyboards**, and choose **Add New Keyboard**.
 
Select **Korean**, choose **Standard** or **10 Key**, and tap **Done**.
 
### What content is included for free?
 
The consonant and double-consonant learning sections are available for free.
 
### What does the Pro upgrade unlock?
 
The one-time Pro upgrade unlocks:
 
- Vowels
- Double vowels
- Putting It Together lessons
- Syllable and word practice
- Korean keyboard exercises
 
### My purchase is not showing. What should I do?
 
Open the upgrade screen and tap **Restore Purchases**.
 
Make sure the iPhone is signed in with the Apple Account used to make the original purchase.
 
### Can I use the app without an internet connection?
 
Most lesson content is stored in the app and can be used offline. An internet connection may be required to make or restore an in-app purchase.
